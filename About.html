Our story

Built loaf by loaf,
from Pretoria Central.

Thuba Rush Kota Joint started the way most great kota spots do: with a small stand, a hot grill, and a neighbourhood that showed up hungry.

History

From street stand to two spots in the city

Thuba Rush Kota Joint opened its doors as a small takeaway counter in Pretoria Central, close to Opera Plaza on Pretorius Street. The idea was simple: give students, office workers and taxi commuters a proper meal they could grab quickly, without paying restaurant prices for it.

Word travelled fast. What began as a single grill and a queue of regulars grew into a second location in Arcadia, and the joint became a fixture on more than one list of Pretoria's best kota spots. Today, both branches still run on the same idea that opened the first one: load it properly, or don't bother.

Mission & vision

Feeding the city's hustle

Our mission is to serve fast, generously loaded, affordably priced food to the people who keep Pretoria moving — students between lectures, staff on a lunch break, and families who want a night off cooking.

Our vision is to be the kota joint every neighbourhood in Pretoria wishes it had on its corner: consistent, welcoming, and never skimping on the fillings.

Our values

The team

Run by people who actually love kotas

Thuba Rush is owned and run by a small, hands-on team who are usually the same friendly faces you'll see behind the grill. Regulars often mention how approachable the owner and staff are — that's on purpose. A kota joint is only as good as the people serving it.
